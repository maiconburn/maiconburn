I am a software engineer with a foundation in marketing, holding a degree from UNOPAR and an MSc in Entrepreneurship from Falmouth University. I combine technical execution with strategic thinking, building systems that are not only functional but purposeful.

Throughout my career, I have contributed to projects for leading Brazilian brands such as BOX1824, Agency Yo, GAD, AG2, MJV, and CUBOCC, delivering high-impact digital solutions across different industries.

Beyond software, I am a 2nd Dan Black Belt in Taekwondo, with competitive experience at the national level in Brazil and the UK. I have also spent seven years teaching children and teenagers, developing discipline, leadership, and resilience, qualities that directly shape how I approach engineering and problem-solving.

I am a founding member of Invalley Innovation and Design and Adeus Rotina, where I lead technology strategy and product development. My work focuses on building systems that create real-world impact, whether through user experience, automation, or scalable digital platforms.

I am driven by the belief that technology should serve humanity. The most meaningful work is not just building tools, but building systems that protect, empower, and elevate people.
